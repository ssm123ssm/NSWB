**Last updated: 29 July 2026**

This notice covers **neurasense.io**, the Neurasense company website. It is short because the site does very little with your data.

## 1. What This Site Collects

**Nothing, unless you contact us.** Browsing neurasense.io does not require an account, and we do not track you across the site or across other sites.

When you submit the contact or access-request form, we collect:

- your **name**;
- your **email address and/or phone number** — at least one is required so we can reply;
- the **subject** you selected;
- any **message** you write;
- the **date and time** of submission.

That is the complete list. Nothing else on this site collects personal information.

## 2. Cookies and Analytics

**This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.** There are no advertising pixels, no tracking scripts, and no third-party measurement tools.

The site stores a single value in your browser's local storage, `ns-theme`, to remember whether you chose the light or dark appearance. It is set only when you use the theme toggle, it never leaves your browser, and it contains nothing but the word `light` or `dark`. You can clear it through your browser's site-data settings.

Because we set no non-essential cookies, this site has no cookie banner and does not need one.

## 3. Why We Use It and On What Basis

We use contact form submissions **solely to respond to your enquiry** and, where relevant, to continue the conversation about working together. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not use your details for marketing unless you separately ask us to.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, our lawful basis is **legitimate interests** — responding to someone who has deliberately contacted us — and, where your enquiry leads towards an agreement, **steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract**. Where Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 applies, we rely on the corresponding basis under that Act.

## 4. Who Sees It

Submissions are delivered to Neurasense staff who handle enquiries, through our internal messaging tool and email provider. Those providers process the data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations, and they do not use it for their own purposes.

**We do not sell your information**, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## 5. How Long We Keep It

Contact enquiries are retained for **24 months** from the last message in the thread, then deleted. Where an enquiry becomes a customer relationship, the resulting records are governed by that product's privacy policy instead.

## 6. Where It Goes

Neurasense is established in Sri Lanka and our providers may operate elsewhere, so your enquiry may be stored and processed outside your country. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA or UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 7. Your Rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of what we hold, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or object to our using it at all. You can also object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests.

Email **hello@neurasense.io** and we will respond within **30 days**. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority — in the EU, the authority where you live or work; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in Sri Lanka, the Data Protection Authority established under the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022.

## 8. Security and Breaches

We use encryption in transit and restrict access to enquiry data to staff who need it. If a breach affecting your information occurs, we will notify the relevant authority within 72 hours where the law requires, and tell you directly where the risk to you is high.

## 9. Children

This site is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

We may update this notice. Material changes will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.
