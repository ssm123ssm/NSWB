Two card-title sizes, and they are not interchangeable

14px/12px is the pair for their compact showcase tiles. Feature cards — the p-6, 240–400px ones — take the 20px title with 16px body. Picking the compact pair for a feature card is the most common way this scale gets misapplied.

Chips

Accent Success Warning Danger Soft Default

Three sizes: sm is 12px type at 4px inline padding, md is 12px, lg is 14px at weight 500 with 12px inline padding. Every semantic hue ships a solid and a soft variant; the soft variant is the hue mixed to 15% with the foreground text sitting at 70–92% of the hue.

Behaviour

Motion

Short, small, and mostly on controls. Surfaces do not move.

Durations

100ms for colour, 250ms for transform, 480ms for the orchestrated section cross-fade. Nothing longer.

Easing

ease-out
cubic-bezier(0, 0, .2, 1)

ease-out-fluid
cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1)

Reduced motion

Handled twice: a prefers-reduced-motion query and a data-reduce-motion attribute, both killing transition-property outright.

The only transforms in the system are scale(0.97) on button press, scale(1.1) on a colour swatch hover, and scale(1.02) on a grouped hover target. There is no card lift, no parallax, and no scroll-driven reveal on the marketing pages.

Addition — not from the reference

The smallest set of fixes that passes

Keep #0485f7 as a fill, where the bar is 3:1 rather than 4.5:1, and darken it to #0067c9 only where it becomes small type. That single substitution clears the accent-as-text and button-label rows without changing what the page looks like at a glance. Semantic hues need the same treatment: use them as fills and dots, not as text.

Working rules

What to do, and what breaks it

The failure modes below are the ones that actually came up building against this system.

Do

Let the border carry the card. On a grey ground, a 1px #dedee0 outline at 24px radius is the whole treatment.

Don't

Reach for a fill gradient or a hue wash to make a card feel designed. It is the specific thing that makes a page read as washed out next to the reference.

Do

Set every heading at weight 500 and let size do the work. The scale runs 72 / 48 / 32 / 20.

Don't

Bold a heading to give it emphasis. There is no 600 or 700 in the system, and one bold heading visibly leaves it.

Do

Put colour in the illustration inside the card, at full saturation.

Don't

Decorate the eyebrow. Theirs is a plain accent line of 16px text with nothing drawn beside it — no dash, no pill, no icon.

Watch

Two patterns look like system components but are not. The dot field belongs to their colour-picker, not to cards; and the --surface shadow token is used on overlays, dialogs and form fields, never on a landing card. Borrowing either onto a card is the most likely way to drift.
